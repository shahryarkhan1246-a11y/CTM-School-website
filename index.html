Govt. CTM High School sits in the Ismailabad Textile Mills Colony area of Multan, a public school that has long served the children of the surrounding colony.

The school prepares students through matriculation under the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Multan, with a focus on steady academic grounding, discipline, and community.

Its courtyards, arched verandas, and painted entrance gate have become part of the neighbourhood's own identity — a school built and shaped by the community around it.

School Guidelinesاسکول کی ہدایات

Rules every student and parent should know.

🕗

School Timing

Gates open 7:00 AM, assembly 8:00 AM sharp. Students arriving after assembly must report to the office.

👕

Uniform

Full uniform is compulsory daily, including proper shoes. Uniform checks happen at the gate.

📗

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is required to sit the annual examination. Leave applications go through the class teacher.

🎒

Books & Supplies

Students must bring the day's textbooks, notebooks, and stationery as per the class timetable.

🤝

Discipline

Respectful conduct toward teachers and fellow students is expected at all times, on and off campus.

💳

Fee Submission

Fees are due in the first week of every month at the school office. Late submissions may incur a fine.
